## What is Xodo?

Xodo is an **all-in-one secure document management and e-signature platform** that provides a complete suite of tools for viewing, editing, converting, organizing, securing, and electronically signing PDFs and other documents. It works across **Web, Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux), and Mobile (iOS/Android)** — with seamless cross-platform sync.

Xodo is positioned as a more affordable alternative to **Adobe Acrobat Pro** (50% less cost) and **DocuSign** for e-signatures.
