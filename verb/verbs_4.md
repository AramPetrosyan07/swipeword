# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 4 (B2)

---

### 1. **PUT OFF** — откладывать (postpone)
> I wouldn't like to **put it off** till tomorrow.
> Never **put off** till tomorrow what you can do today.

---

### 2. **GO OFF** — испортиться (go bad, spoil)
> The food can **go off**.
> All the food **went off**.

---

### 3. **LET DOWN** — подвести (disappoint)
> I know that he will never **let us down**.
> If my memory doesn't **let me down**.

---

### 4. **DROP OUT OF** — бросить учёбу (drop out)
> To everyone's surprise, he **dropped out of** college.

---

### 5. **TAKE OFF** — взлетать (take off); снимать (одежду)
> The plane will **take off** in an hour.

---

### 6. **TALK OVER** — обговорить, обсудить (discuss)
> I need to **talk it over** with you.

---

### 7. **GO UP** — вырастать, подниматься (rise)
> The price **went up** to our surprise.
> The cost of this apartment **went up** by 10% last year.

---

### 8. **GO DOWN** — снижаться (fall, decrease)
> The price of oil **went down** by more than 30% recently.
> Her income **went down** by 20%.

---

### 9. **PUT UP WITH** — мириться с (tolerate)
> She can't **put up with** this anymore.
> He can't **put up with** this injustice.

---

### 10. **PUT UP** — повысить (цену) (raise, increase)
> They decided to **put up** the price.
> The company was forced to **put up** the price.

---

### 11. **GO BY** — проходить (о времени) (pass)
> Time **goes by** so quickly.
> It is a well-known fact that time **goes by** so quickly.

---

### 12. **THINK OVER** — обдумать (consider)
> She said that she would need some time to **think over** their proposal.

---

### 13. **POINT OUT** — отмечать, указывать (point out)
> I'd like to **point out** that this is worth our attention.
> I'd like to **point out** that this is really important.
> The teacher **pointed out** these mistakes.
