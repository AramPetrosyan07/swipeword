# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 6 (B2)

---

### 1. **CLOSE DOWN** — закрыться (о магазине, компании) (shut down)
> This company will have to **close down**.
> The company had to **close down** because of a very difficult economic situation.

---

### 2. **BRING DOWN** — снижать (цену) (reduce)
> The company decided to **bring down** the price.

---

### 3. **BACK UP** — поддерживать; создавать резервную копию (support, back up)
> I'll **back you up** when you need it.
> I need to make sure I **backed up** everything.
> Make sure you **backed up** all the files.

---

### 4. **PUT ASIDE** — отложить, отбросить в сторону (set aside)
> **Put aside** all your fears.

---

### 5. **PUT DOWN** — унижать, оскорблять (humiliate)
> Stop **putting him down**.

---

### 6. **CALM DOWN** — успокоиться (calm down)
> **Calm down!**
> You need to **calm down** a little.
> I'll try to **calm her down**.

---

### 7. **COOL DOWN** — успокоиться, остыть (cool down)
> **Cool down!**
> He **cooled down** and realized that he was wrong.
> You need to **cool down** and look at it more objectively.

---

### 8. **WRITE DOWN** — записать (write down)
> I'll **write down** this word.
> I'll **write this word down**.

---

### 9. **COME ALONG** — пойти с кем-то, присоединиться (come along, accompany)
> You can **come along** with me.
> Do you want to **come along**?

---

### 10. **CHECK IN** — зарегистрироваться (в отеле, аэропорту) (check in)
> I **checked in** at the hotel.
> I have already **checked in** at the hotel.
> When are you going to **check in**?

---

### 11. **CHECK OUT** — выезжать (из отеля) (check out)
> What time will he **check out**?

---

### 12. **BREAK OUT** — разразиться, вспыхнуть (break out)
> The fire **broke out** suddenly.

---

### 13. **DRESS DOWN** — одеваться неформально (dress casually)
> Some office workers **dress down** on Fridays.

---

### 14. **CALL FOR** — требовать (require, demand)
> This type of work **calls for** more skills and professionalism.
> This **calls for** extra effort.
> This kind of work **calls for** more concentration.
