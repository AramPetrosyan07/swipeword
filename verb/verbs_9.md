# DEPENDENT PREPOSITIONS (ЗАВИСИМЫЕ ПРЕДЛОГИ) — Lesson 9 (B2)

---

### 1. **HOPE FOR** — надеяться на (hope for)
> We **hope for** further cooperation.
> We **hope for** fruitful cooperation with you.

---

### 2. **SYMPATHY FOR** — сочувствие к (sympathy for)
> He doesn't feel **sympathy for** those people who are in real need.

---

### 3. **SMILE AT** — улыбаться кому-то (smile at)
> He **smiled at** her and waved.

---

### 4. **DEPEND ON** — зависеть от (depend on)
> He doesn't want to **depend on** his parents any longer.

---

### 5. **SUFFER FROM** — страдать от (suffer from)
> He often **suffers from** depression.
> She **suffers from** constant headaches.

---

### 6. **LEAD TO** — привести к (lead to)
> This **led** the company **to** bankruptcy.
> Do you agree that hard work **leads** us **to** success?
> This can **lead to** unforeseen circumstances.

---

### 7. **INVITATION TO** — приглашение на (invitation to)
> I'll send you an **invitation to** the meeting.

---

### 8. **BUSY WITH** — занят чем-то (busy with)
> They are **busy with** a very important project.

---

### 9. **CONTRIBUTION TO** — вклад во что-то (contribution to)
> He made a very significant **contribution to** our society.
> He made a substantial **contribution to** charity.

---

### 10. **ACCUSE OF** — обвинять в (accuse of)
> He was unfairly **accused of** that crime.

---

### 11. **RESISTANCE TO** — сопротивление чему-то (resistance to)
> There was so much **resistance to** this new law.

---

### 12. **INCREASE IN** — рост / увеличение (increase in)
> There was a sudden **increase in** sales.
> They predicted a huge **increase in** sales.

---

### 13. **DECREASE IN** — снижение (decrease in)
> There was an unexpected **decrease in** sales.
