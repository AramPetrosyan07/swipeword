# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 3 (B2)

---

### 1. **COME IN (FOR)** — зайти, заглянуть (drop in)
> Would you like to **come in for** a cup of coffee?
> You can **come in for** a cup of tea.

---

### 2. **COME ROUND** — заглянуть, навестить (come over)
> If you don't mind, I'll **come round** to you for a cup of tea.

---

### 3. **TAKE AFTER** — быть похожим на (resemble)
> He **takes after** his father.
> She doesn't **take after** his sister at all.

---

### 4. **LOOK AFTER** — присматривать, ухаживать (take care of)
> He **looks after** his old grandmother.

---

### 5. **LOOK OUT / WATCH OUT** — смотреть по сторонам, остерегаться (watch out, be careful)
> **Look out!**
> **Watch out**, or you can fall.

---

### 6. **BRING UP** — воспитывать (raise)
> He was born and **brought up** in the States.
> He was **brought up** by his uncle.

---

### 7. **FALL OUT** — ссориться (quarrel)
> He tries not to **fall out** with others.

---

### 8. **LOOK THROUGH** — просмотреть, пролистать (browse through)
> I'll **look through** these documents.

---

### 9. **FALL BEHIND** — отставать (fall behind)
> If you don't do anything urgent, you can **fall behind** very easily.
> She **fell behind** the rest of the group.

---

### 10. **LOOK BACK (ON)** — оглядываться (на) (look back on)
> When she **looks back on** those days, she realizes how happy she was.
> **Looking back on** my life, I come to the conclusion that many mistakes were made.

---

### 11. **LOOK AHEAD** — смотреть вперёд, забегать вперёд (look ahead)
> **Looking ahead**, we need to be ready for this.

---

### 12. **PUT ACROSS** — донести, передать (мысль) (convey, put across)
> I'll try to **put across** my idea.
> I tried to **put across** this point.

---

### 13. **GET BACK TO** — возвращаться к (get back to)
> Let's **get back to** discussing our work.
> Let's **get back to** discussing this.
