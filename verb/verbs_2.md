# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 2 (B2)

---

### 1. **GET ON WITH / GET ALONG WITH** — ладить с кем-то (get along with)
> I **get on well with** my neighbors.
> We **get on well with** each other.
> My colleagues and I **get along well with** each other.

---

### 2. **CATCH UP WITH** — догнать кого-то (catch up with)
> I'll try to **catch up with** the others.
> I can't **catch up with** you.
> I'll try to **catch up with** you.

---

### 3. **KEEP UP WITH** — поспевать, не отставать (keep up with)
> It was very difficult for him to **keep up with** the rest of the class.

---

### 4. **BE OVER** — закончиться (finish, be over)
> It will **be over** soon.
> The game will **be over** soon.

---

### 5. **COME ACROSS** — встретить, натолкнуться (come across, encounter)
> I **came across** so many new words in this text.
> I have never **come across** this word.
> I have never **come across** this expression.

---

### 6. **CARRY ON** — продолжать (carry on, continue)
> We can't **carry on** doing this.
> I'll **carry on** doing this.

---

### 7. **RUN OUT OF** — закончиться (run out of)
> She **ran out of** money.
> We've just **run out of** time.
> As I see, we are **running out of** time.
> We need to hurry up because we are **running out of** time.

---

### 8. **LOOK DOWN ON** — смотреть свысока (look down on, disrespect)
> Some of his colleagues **look down on** him.

---

### 9. **GET DOWN** — расстраивать, угнетать (get down, depress)
> These problems sometimes **get me down**.
> This daily routine **gets her down**.

---

### 10. **GET DOWN TO** — приступить к (get down to, start)
> I need to **get down to** work.
> Let's **get down to** business.

---

### 11. **FIND OUT** — выяснить, узнать (find out)
> She wants to **find out** the whole truth.
