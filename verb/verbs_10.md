# DEPENDENT PREPOSITIONS (ЗАВИСИМЫЕ ПРЕДЛОГИ) — Lesson 10 (B2)

---

### 1. **DISAGREE WITH / ON** — не соглашаться с кем-л. / по поводу (disagree with/on)
> They **disagree on** this issue.
> He **disagrees with** his colleagues **on** many issues.

---

### 2. **ADVANTAGES OF** — преимущества чего-то (advantages of)
> This is one of the main **advantages of** this profession.
> This is one of the main **advantages of** living in this city.

---

### 3. **DISADVANTAGES OF** — недостатки чего-то (disadvantages of)
> This is one of the **disadvantages of** this job.
> What are the **advantages and disadvantages of** distance learning?

---

### 4. **ABSENCE FROM** — отсутствие на (absence from)
> How did he explain his **absence from** work?

---

### 5. **COMPENSATE FOR** — компенсировать что-то (compensate for)
> They must **compensate** you **for** these losses.

---

### 6. **DEMAND FOR** — спрос на (demand for)
> There is a growing **demand for** these products.
> There is a growing **demand for** cheap housing.

---

### 7. **APOLOGIZE TO** — извиняться перед кем-то (apologize to)
> He went and **apologized to** her.

---

### 8. **APOLOGIZE FOR** — извиняться за что-то (apologize for)
> They **apologized for** the delay.
> I'd like to **apologize for** coming late.

---

### 9. **STARE AT** — уставиться на (stare at)
> The stranger was **staring at** him.
> Everyone was **staring at** her.

---

### 10. **INTERFERE IN** — вмешиваться во что-то (interfere in)
> I think he shouldn't **interfere in** other people's affairs.

---

### 11. **CURE FOR** — лечение от (сущ.) (cure for)
> A **cure for** this disease hasn't been found yet.
> There is no **cure for** this disease.

---

### 12. **CURE OF** — излечить от (глаг.) (cure of)
> He was **cured of** this disease.
