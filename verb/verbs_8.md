# DEPENDENT PREPOSITIONS (ЗАВИСИМЫЕ ПРЕДЛОГИ) — Lesson 8 (B2)

---

### 1. **INVEST IN** — вкладывать, инвестировать в (invest in)
> He **invested** his money **in** a very risky project.

---

### 2. **SURPRISED AT / BY** — удивлён чем-то (surprised at/by)
> I was **surprised at / by** his reaction to these words.

---

### 3. **NEED FOR** — необходимость в (need for)
> There was no **need for** such radical changes.

---

### 4. **MANAGE TO** — суметь, удалось (manage to)
> How did you **manage to** enter the room?

---

### 5. **AWARE OF** — осведомлён о, осознавать (aware of)
> The company is fully **aware of** this problem.
> I am fully **aware of** the fact that it takes time.

---

### 6. **INSIST ON** — настаивать на (insist on)
> Our director **insists on** punctuality.

---

### 7. **ABSORBED IN** — поглощён чем-то (absorbed in)
> He is completely **absorbed in** the movie.

---

### 8. **ENGROSSED IN** — поглощён чем-то (engrossed in)
> He is **engrossed in** the book.

---

### 9. **SHOUT AT** — кричать на (shout at)
> He needs to stop **shouting at** her.

---

### 10. **CONSIST OF** — состоять из (consist of)
> The cake **consists of** flour, sugar and eggs.
> The book **consists of** 10 chapters.

---

### 11. **IMPRESSED BY / WITH** — поражён, изумлён (impressed by/with)
> I was really **impressed by** the result.
> I was really **impressed by** the news.

---

### 12. **REGISTER FOR** — зарегистрироваться на (register for)
> Have you already **registered for** the course?
> I'm going to **register for** the course.

---

### 13. **COMPLAIN ABOUT** — жаловаться на (complain about)
> Did he **complain about** it?
> He **complains about** his salary because he is often underpaid.

---

### 14. **KEY TO** — ключ к (key to)
> What is the **key to** success?
> Hard work is the **key to** success.

---

### 15. **EXCITED ABOUT** — взволнован, в восторге от (excited about)
> I'm so **excited about** the upcoming event.
> She is **excited about** the upcoming concert.

---

### 16. **APPLY TO** — подать заявление в (apply to)
> She **applied to** this university and was accepted.
