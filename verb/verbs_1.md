# PHRASAL VERBS — выделенные фразовые глаголы

---

### 1. **CALL OFF** — отменить (cancel)
> They **called off** the game.
> The game was **called off** because of the weather.
> The conference has just been **called off**.
> It was decided to **call off** this event.

---

### 2. **FIGURE OUT** — понимать, разобраться (understand)
> I can't **figure out** how it works.
> He can't **figure out** what to do.

---

### 3. **LOOK UP TO** — восхищаться, уважать (admire, respect)
> I really **look up to** him.
> I **look up to** such a person.
> Not surprisingly, most people **look up to** him because he is a real hero.

---

### 4. **GO ON** — продолжать (continue); происходить (happen)
> He can't **go on** pretending that everything is okay.
> My friend **went on** talking about it.
> I would like to **go on** studying English.
> What's **going on** here?

---

### 5. **FLICK THROUGH** — просмотреть, пролистать (browse)
> I will **flick through** this document.
> I took one book and **flicked through** it.

---

### 6. **SEE OFF** — провожать (see someone off)
> I'll **see you off** after lessons.
> He **saw her off** after the party.

---

### 7. **TAKE UP** — начать заниматься (start doing)
> I **took up** studying English seriously.
> I **took up** jogging.

---

### 8. **LOOK INTO** — изучать, расследовать (investigate)
> My colleague wants to **look into** this problem more thoroughly.
> The police are **looking into** this crime.

---

### 9. **MAKE UP FOR** — компенсировать (compensate)
> Who will **make up for** these losses?
> I'll try to **make up for** lost time.
> I don't think they will be able to **make up for** these losses so quickly.

---

### 10. **GIVE UP** — сдаваться; бросать (quit)
> Never **give up**.
> He **gave up** smoking a couple of years ago.

---

### 11. **THROW AWAY** — выбрасывать (throw away)
> She **threw it away**.
> I don't need it, so you can **throw it away**.

---

### 12. **LOOK UP** — искать (в словаре) (look up)
> I'll **look it up** in a dictionary.
> You can **look it up** in a dictionary.

---

### 13. **ASK OUT** — приглашать на свидание (ask out)
> He decided to **ask her out**.
> He was so happy to **ask her out**.

---

### 14. **LEAVE OUT** — упускать, не включать (omit)
> Some very important details were **left out**.
> It is seen that the most important details were **left out**.
