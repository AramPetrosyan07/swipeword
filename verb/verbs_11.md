# PHRASAL VERBS — зависимые предлоги (Dependent Prepositions)

---

### 1. **SUITABLE FOR** — подходит для (suitable for)
> This apartment is **suitable for** a small family.
> This documentary is perfectly **suitable for** people of all ages.

---

### 2. **BORED WITH** — надоело, наскучило (bored with)
> He says that he is really **bored with** his present job.
> The kids got **bored with** the game.

---

### 3. **PROVIDE WITH** — предоставлять (provide with)
> He **provides** us **with** very valuable information.
> The company **provides** very good conditions **for** its employees.

---

### 4. **DELIGHTED WITH** — в восторге от (delighted with)
> I was **delighted with** this result.

---

### 5. **DELIGHTED TO** — в восторге делать что-то (delighted to)
> I'm absolutely **delighted to** see you.
> I'm absolutely **delighted to** be here.
> I'm absolutely **delighted to** hear that.

---

### 6. **PLEASED TO** — рад (pleased to)
> I'm **pleased to** meet you.

---

### 7. **DEPEND ON** — зависеть от (depend on)
> He **depends on** his parents.
> He strongly **depends on** his parents.

---

### 8. **FAMILIAR TO** — знаком кому-то (familiar to)
> This place doesn't look **familiar to** me.

---

### 9. **OPTIMISTIC ABOUT** — оптимистичный по поводу (optimistic about)
> He is very **optimistic about** the future of the company.

---

### 10. **CONFIDENT OF** — уверен в (confident of)
> I'm absolutely **confident of** that.

---

### 11. **DANGER OF** — опасность чего-то (danger of)
> There is a **danger of** new protests.
> There is a **danger of** a new crisis.

---

### 12. **KNOWN FOR** — известен чем-то (known for)
> He is **known for** his honesty.
> This city is **known for** its rich history.
> He is **known for** his kindness.

---

### 13. **LACK OF** — недостаток, нехватка (lack of)
> They demonstrated a **lack of** knowledge and experience.
> I know that I have a **lack of** confidence, but I'm trying to work on this problem.

---

### 14. **LACK** — не хватать (to lack)
> I know that I **lack** confidence, but I'm trying to work on this problem.
