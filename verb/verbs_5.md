# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 5 (B2)

---

### 1. **COUNT ON** — рассчитывать на (rely on)
> You can **count on** my help.
> You can **count on** my support.

---

### 2. **GET BY (ON)** — прожить на, обходиться (manage on)
> It's almost impossible to **get by on** such a low salary.
> How does he **get by on** such a small income?

---

### 3. **MAKE UP** — придумывать (invent)
> She **made up** this story.
> You need to **make up** your own sentence.

---

### 4. **GET AWAY** — сбежать (escape)
> The robbers didn't manage to **get away**.

---

### 5. **PUT FORWARD** — выдвигать, предлагать (propose)
> A new interesting suggestion was **put forward**.
> They **put forward** a very interesting idea.

---

### 6. **PUT OUT** — потушить (extinguish)
> **Put out** the fire.

---

### 7. **MAKE OUT** — понимать, различать (make out, understand)
> I can't **make out** what's going on.
> I can't **make out** what she's talking about.
> As far as I can **make out**, we need to do the following.

---

### 8. **CARRY OUT** — проводить (conduct, carry out)
> I'm going to **carry out** this research.
> They **carried out** a very interesting experiment.

---

### 9. **GIVE AWAY** — раздавать, отдавать (give away)
> He **gave away** his old clothes.
> He **gave** the money **away** to charity.

---

### 10. **PICK UP** — забрать (pick up, collect)
> He'll **pick you up** after school.
> I'll **pick you up** after the meeting is over.

---

### 11. **CUT DOWN ON** — уменьшить потребление (reduce, cut down on)
> He decided to **cut down on** smoking.
> She wants to **cut down on** eating chocolate.

---

### 12. **CHEER UP** — взбодриться, поднять настроение (cheer up)
> **Cheer up!**
> I suppose this news will **cheer you up**.
