# PHRASAL VERBS — Lesson 7 (B2)

---

### 1. **EAT OUT** — есть не дома (dine out)
> Do you like **eating out**?
> Let's **eat out** tonight.

---

### 2. **COME ON** — давай, ну же (come on, encourage)
> **Come on**, try it again.
> **Come on**, let's do it.

---

### 3. **DO UP** — застегнуть (fasten); ремонтировать (renovate)
> He **did up** his jacket.
> **Do up** your belt.
> She **did up** her apartment last summer.
> He bought an old house and **did it up**.

---

### 4. **MOVE ON (TO)** — перейти к (proceed to)
> I **moved on to** the next exercise.
> Let's **move on to** the next question.

---

### 5. **TAKE IN** — обманывать (deceive)
> They want to **take her in**.
> This woman was **taken in**.

---

### 6. **GIVE OFF** — издавать (запах) (emit)
> The food **gives off** a bad smell.
> That food **gives off** a very unpleasant smell.

---

### 7. **GIVE OUT** — раздавать (distribute)
> The teacher **gave out** these materials.

---

### 8. **BRING ABOUT** — вызывать, приводить к (cause, lead to)
> The government **brought about** a new law.
> This **brought about** profound changes in the lives of ordinary people.

---

### 9. **COME THROUGH** — перенести, пройти через (endure, survive)
> He **came through** a very complicated operation.
> He couldn't imagine what I **came through**.

---

### 10. **GO UNDER** — разориться (go bankrupt)
> The company will **go under** if the situation doesn't improve.

---

### 11. **BRIGHTEN UP** — скрасить, украсить (brighten up)
> This **brightens up** my life.
> It **brightens up** my life.
